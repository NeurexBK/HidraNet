List a .hidra site

Read this before you publish. On HidraNet the address is the decryption key — that is how a site can live on relays that cannot read it. Listing it here puts it in a public directory, so anyone will be able to open the site. That is what you want for a forum or a public page, and it cannot be taken back: once the address is out, it stays out. A site meant for a few people does not belong in this directory.
This is where every review is sent, encrypted. Open the Mail app in HidraNet, copy your address and paste it here. (Mail has to be opened at least once to receive.)
