Summary

In one paragraph

HidraNet is an open-source privacy network: a browser, encrypted applications, its own .hidra services and HidraVPN, all under the MIT licence. HNET is its token on Solana. It launched with 1,000,000,000 and no presale; burns have taken the supply to 957,402,743, and no one can ever add to it. HNET pays for things that cost money to run: servers and bandwidth today, HidraVPN access and the other paid HidraNet services as they arrive. It also lets people buy and sell on .hidra. Privacy itself is never paywalled: the network, the browser and the apps stay free.

Each use of HNET below carries a label: Live works today, Planned is on the roadmap, and planned is not a promise of a date.

2

What HNET is for

Fund the network Live

HNET sent to the support wallet pays for servers, bandwidth and releases. An independent security audit is the next planned use; the auditor will be named when hired. Every transfer is public on chain.

Buy and sell on .hidra Live, basic

Shops published as .hidra sites take HNET straight into the seller's wallet, and HidraReview lists them under Marketplace. An integrated marketplace, with signed listings and encrypted orders, is planned.

Pay for HidraVPN and HidraNet services Planned

HidraVPN access first, then the other paid HidraNet services, paid in HNET. VPN access uses passes built on blind signatures: the issuer sees a payment and the VPN node sees a pass, and nothing links the two. Before this goes live we will publish who runs the issuer and how nodes stop a pass being spent twice. Free access stays.

Bounties and HidraNet Pro Planned

Rewards in HNET for reported security flaws, and paid extras that cost money to run (more HidraMail storage, more .hidra sites), payable in HNET.

4

What HNET is not

  • Not a share. It gives no claim on HidraNet, its assets, its revenue or its profit.
  • Not a source of income. No staking rewards, interest, dividends or revenue share.
  • Not a price promise. No buyback, no burn schedule, no price support.
  • Not a gate. The network, HidraBrowser and the apps never require HNET.
  • Not a vote. The code is MIT-licensed; anyone may run it, audit it and fork it.
5

Buying safely

Check the mint, not the name. Anyone can create a token called HNET. The real one is Aov3BtQvrGXM5V9oiHu8PKJWeV8a2rFnr24fwWcNpump; compare it character by character.

Never share a seed phrase or a private key. HidraNet never asks for one, anywhere.

We never write first. No one from HidraNet starts a private conversation to offer help, an airdrop or a sale.
