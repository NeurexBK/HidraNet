Using HidraNet Browser

How to connect, chat, send mail, publish a .hidra site and stay safe. Button names are quoted as they appear on screen.

1. Getting started

Connect and confirm

When you open the browser, the Tor connection starts by itself and the new tab page shows the progress. Wait until it reaches 100% and an address appears under Exit IP. Until then, the browser holds web traffic back rather than let it leave unprotected.

If the circuit is ready but no exit IP appears, the browser could not confirm the address with check.torproject.org. The browser will not promise what it has not verified; try again with New identity.

New identity

New identity, in Anonymity Settings, asks Tor for a new circuit. Your next requests leave through another relay, with another address. Use it to separate one activity from the one before.

Tabs

Every website tab is a sealed compartment: its own cookies, its own memory and its own fingerprint. What you do in one tab is not visible from another, and when you close the tab, everything it stored is deleted. If you log into an account in one tab, the other tabs are not logged in.

What the browser refuses on its own

Requests for location, camera, microphone, notifications and MIDI are denied without asking you. WebRTC, which can reveal your real address, is held to the proxy.

Being counted (optional)

Count me on the network, in Anonymity Settings, is off by default. If you turn it on, the browser sends a random code, its version and the time every 30 seconds, so the project can count how many browsers are running. It sends no location and nothing about what you do.

2. HidraChat

End-to-end encrypted chat rooms. Content is encrypted on your computer before it leaves, and only people with the room password can read it.

Joining a room

  1. Open HidraChat from the new tab page.
  2. Type your nickname.
  3. Type the room password, agreed beforehand with the people you will talk to. The same password always leads to the same room.
  4. Press Join room.

Golden rules

  • Agree on the password through another channel, in person if possible. Anyone with the password reads everything.
  • A long password is far safer than a short one: use at least four unrelated words.
  • Recent rooms appear in a list, so you can rejoin with one click.

Good to know

In this version, encrypted messages travel through public message brokers run by third parties. They can see that traffic exists, but cannot read it.

3. HidraMail

Mail between network addresses, end-to-end encrypted and signed by the sender. Each message is encrypted to the recipient's public key with a key made for that message only.

Creating an address

  1. Open HidraMail.
  2. Press Generate a new anonymous address.
  3. The address appears under My addresses. Use Copy to share it.

You can have several addresses and use one per context.

Sending and receiving

Compose opens a new message. Fill in the recipient, the subject and the text, and press Send. Received messages appear in the Inbox; Reply, Spam and Delete are at the top of each message.

Good to know

Your addresses' private keys exist only on your computer, sealed with your system's keychain where there is one. If you delete the browser data, you lose the addresses and the messages: there is no recovery, because nobody else holds the keys.

4. SevenNine: your own .hidra site

Create

Open SevenNine and choose a name with letters, numbers, hyphen or underscore, up to 32 characters. The site gets a readable address, such as mysite.hidra, and a 40-character cryptographic address derived from the site's key.

Edit

The visual editor composes pages, previews them in phone format and uploads files: up to 2 MB per page and 5 MB of files in total.

Publish

  1. Press Publish to the network.
  2. Under 1. Choose the site, pick the site.
  3. Under 2. Publish, confirm.
  4. The site appears under My published sites. Use Copy to share its address.

Good to know

The readable address is resolved on your computer and is not unique on the network: someone else may use the same name. To be found unambiguously, share the cryptographic address.

5. Forum

Public discussions, organised in categories. Only your nickname appears. New topic opens a discussion: pick the category, write the title and message, and press Post topic. Reply answers an existing topic.

Everything posted on the forum is public and stored on third-party message brokers. Do not post anything that could identify you.

6. HidraSearch

Search that queries several engines, such as DuckDuckGo, SearXNG and Brave Search, always through Tor. The engine sees the request coming from a Tor exit relay, not from your address. The browser keeps no search history; each engine's logging policy is its own.

7. Good practice

  • Wait for 100% and an exit IP before browsing.
  • Use different tabs for different identities.
  • Do not log into personal accounts in the session where you want to stay anonymous.
  • Keep the browser updated: new versions fix security flaws.
  • If you use HidraMail, keep an encrypted backup of the data folder.
  • Be careful with downloaded files: a document opened outside the browser may connect to the internet directly, without Tor.
