# Ambassador Programme — HidraNet

**Version 1.0 · September 2026**

---

## 1. What it is

A programme for people who want to help HidraNet grow — explaining it,
translating it, organising meet-ups, supporting newcomers, running nodes.

**Principle:** HidraNet grows through trust between people, not through
advertising. Someone who already uses it and explains it to a friend is worth
more than any campaign.

---

## 2. What an ambassador does

Pick what suits you. There is no need to do everything.

| Area | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Outreach** | Writing articles, recording videos, speaking at events |
| **Community** | Answering questions, supporting newcomers, moderating |
| **Translation** | Translating documentation and the interface |
| **Infrastructure** | Running nodes, documenting how to run them |
| **Training** | Workshops, tutorials, open sessions |
| **Feedback** | Testing new versions, reporting problems |

---

## 3. Levels

| Level | Requirements | Benefits |
|---|---|---|
| **Supporter** | Use it and talk about it | Access to the ambassadors' channel · Badge |
| **Ambassador** | 3 months of regular activity · 1 significant contribution | All of the above · Early builds · A place on the community page · Merchandise |
| **Senior Ambassador** | 12 months · sustained, recognised contribution | All of the above · A say in product decisions · A budget for local events · A reference for opportunities |

**There is no competition and no limited number of places.** Whoever meets the
criteria moves up.

## 4. How to apply

📧 **ambassadors@hidranet.org**

Tell us:
1. Who you are (a pseudonym is fine)
2. How you came across HidraNet
3. What you would like to do
4. How much time you can realistically give
5. Relevant links, if any

**We do not require real identification.** It would contradict the project.

A response within 14 days.

### 5.1 On payment

**The programme is not paid in money.** We would rather say so now than create an
expectation.

HidraNet is an early-stage project with limited resources. When there is
capacity, we may create grants for specific contributions. Senior ambassadors
have priority for any job opportunities that arise.

Promising vague future payment is a form of exploitation we do not want to
practise.

## 6. What we ask

### 6.1 Honesty about the product

**The most important rule of the programme.**

| Do not say | Say |
|---|---|
| "HidraNet makes you 100% anonymous" | "HidraNet hides your IP from sites; no tool gives absolute anonymity" |
| "Impossible to trace" | "It makes tracing much harder; see the threat model" |
| "Better than Tor" | "Different from Tor: it brings integrated applications; Tor Browser has more years of auditing" |
| "Completely secure" | "In active development, with an external audit planned" |

**Why this matters so much.** If someone trusts an exaggerated promise and
suffers real consequences, the moral responsibility lies with whoever
exaggerated. In a privacy tool, overselling is not merely bad taste — it can
carry a human cost.

Always prefer to under-promise.

### 6.2 Respect the Code of Conduct

It applies in full, including the rule never to attempt to deanonymise anyone.

### 6.3 Do not speak for the company

You may say that you are an ambassador. You may not make statements on behalf of
HidraNet, Lda, or enter into commitments.

A useful formula: *"I am a HidraNet ambassador. My opinion is my own."*

### 6.4 No promotion of unlawful activity

HidraNet is a privacy tool for ordinary people. We do not promote, and do not
tolerate promoting, its use for unlawful purposes.

### 6.5 No political confrontation

HidraNet is a technical tool. The programme is not a platform for political
activism or for confrontation with authorities or governments. Privacy is a
right, and we defend it in technical and legal terms, not political ones.

## 8. Ending the role

The status ends by:

- Your own request, at any time
- Inactivity for more than 6 months, with prior notice
- A breach of the Code of Conduct
- Repeated misleading statements about the product's capabilities
- Misuse of the brand

Ending it is not a penalty — it is keeping the list accurate. Anyone inactive can
come back.

## 9. Frequently asked questions

**Do I need to know how to program?**
No. Outreach, translation, community support and organising events are as useful
as code.

**Can I be an ambassador under a pseudonym?**
Yes. We do not ask for real identification.

**How much time do I have to give?**
There is no fixed minimum. Ambassador level implies regular activity, but
"regular" is compatible with a few hours a month.

**Can I create content in any language?**
Yes, and it is encouraged.

**Can I monetise content about HidraNet?**
Yes — articles, videos, courses. We only ask that you state that you are an
ambassador and that you respect the honesty rules in section 6.1.
