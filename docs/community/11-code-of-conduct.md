# Code of Conduct — HidraNet

Adapted from the Contributor Covenant v2.1

---

## 1. Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse and
healthy community.

---

## 2. Expected behaviour

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility for our own mistakes, apologising to those affected,
  and learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best for the community, not only for ourselves

**In code review**, specifically:

- Critique the code, never the person
- Explain the **why**, not only what to change
- Clearly separate personal preference from a real problem
- Acknowledge what is well done, not only what needs fixing
- Assume good faith and competence

---

## 3. Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery; sexual attention or advances
- Insulting or derogatory comments; personal or political attacks
- Harassment, public or private
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission —
  including physical addresses, email addresses or real identities
- Stalking, inside or outside the project's spaces
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

### 3.1 Rules specific to this project

HidraNet's nature requires three additional rules:

**Never attempt to deanonymise anyone.** Publicly speculating about a
participant's real identity, location or circumstances — even out of curiosity or
as a joke — is a serious violation. Many people contribute to privacy projects
under a pseudonym for serious reasons.

**Respect pseudonymity.** Contributing under a pseudonym is legitimate and
requires no justification. Do not ask anyone for their real name.

**Do not use the project to promote unlawful activity.** HidraNet is privacy
software. The project's spaces are not a place to promote or coordinate illegal
activity.

---

## 4. Scope

This Code applies in all the project's spaces — the repository, issues, pull
requests, discussions, communication channels — and when someone represents the
project publicly.

---

## 5. Reporting violations

📧 **conduct@hidranet.org**

Reports are reviewed promptly and fairly. **The privacy and safety of the person
reporting are respected.**

If the report involves one of the maintainers, you may address it to the other
directly.

### What to include

1. What happened, with links or screenshots if they exist
2. When and where
3. Whether the situation is ongoing
4. How we can contact you

You do not need exhaustive evidence in order to report.

---

## 6. Consequences

### 6.1 Correction

**Impact:** inappropriate language or conduct considered unprofessional.
**Consequence:** a private written warning, with an explanation. A public apology
may be requested.

### 6.2 Warning

**Impact:** a violation through one incident or a series of actions.
**Consequence:** a warning with consequences for continued conduct. No
interaction with the people involved for a set period. A further violation may
lead to suspension or a ban.

### 6.3 Temporary suspension

**Impact:** a serious violation, including sustained inappropriate conduct.
**Consequence:** suspension from any public interaction with the project for a
set period.

### 6.4 Permanent ban

**Impact:** a pattern of violation, harassment, aggression or disparagement of
groups.
**Consequence:** a permanent ban from any public interaction.

### 6.5 Immediate permanent ban

These apply without prior stages:

- Attempting to deanonymise a participant
- Publishing a third party's personal information
- Credible threats of violence
- Sexual harassment

---

## 7. Appeals

Anyone who disagrees with a decision may appeal within **30 days** to
**appeals@hidranet.org**, setting out the grounds.

The appeal is reviewed by someone not involved in the original decision. A
response within 14 days.
