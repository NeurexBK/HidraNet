# 11 — Jurisdiction Comparison

Requested: a comparison of the three, with a recommendation.

---

## 2. Analysis

### Portugal — Lda

**For:**
- Fast, cheap incorporation; *Empresa na Hora* does it in hours.
- **Full eligibility for European funds** — NGI Zero, Horizon Europe, EIC
  Accelerator, Portugal 2030, IAPMEI.
- Native GDPR, which is the framework the product wants to be in.
- Startup Portugal certification, R&D tax incentives (SIFIDE).
- **Real substance** if the founders live here — no tax residence questions.

**Against:**
- Tax and social contribution burden above Estonia's.
- More day-to-day bureaucracy than Estonia.
- Small domestic market for HidraReview.

### Brazil — LTDA

**For:**
- **The HidraReview market is an order of magnitude larger.** Millions of
  micro-businesses, a strong online review culture, fast adoption of digital
  payments.
- Same language, product already in Portuguese.

**Against, and it is decisive:**
- **It eliminates NGI Zero, Horizon Europe and the EIC Accelerator.** These
  programmes require an entity or resident in a Member State or associated
  country. You lose access to €21.6M (Commons Fund) + €9.6M (Entrust) of
  non-dilutive money for exactly this kind of project.
- 30–90 days to incorporate, apostilles, local representation.
- Higher incorporation and compliance costs.
- The Marco Civil da Internet adds retention obligations that are
  philosophically in conflict with an anonymity network.

### Estonia — OÜ (e-Residency)

**For:**
- Entirely remote incorporation and management.
- Corporate income tax **deferred until profits are distributed** — good for
  reinvestment.
- EU jurisdiction: eligible for European funds.
- Strong digital culture; common in privacy projects.

**Against, and it is not small:**
- **Tax residence risk.** If the founders live and work in Portugal, the
  company's effective management is in Portugal, and the Portuguese tax authority
  may treat it as tax resident in Portugal — regardless of the Estonian
  registration. You end up with two sets of filing obligations instead of an
  advantage. **It is the most common e-Residency mistake.**
- No local substance: the Estonian authorities expect some real nexus.
- Estonian banking is harder for non-residents; accounts via fintechs, with
  additional restrictions on privacy activities (see
  [10, section 5](10-legal-and-regulatory-risk.md)).

---

## 3. Recommendation

### ✅ **Portugal, Lda**

**Main reason, and it is sufficient on its own:** the first money into this
project should be non-dilutive, and **all the relevant non-dilutive money
requires an EU entity or resident**. NGI Zero funds precisely open-source
decentralised privacy networks — HidraNet already meets the technical and licence
(MIT) requirements. Incorporating outside the EU closes that door to start a
company in a jurisdiction we do not need.

**Secondary reasons:**
- Real substance where the founders are, with no dual tax residence risk.
- Incorporation in hours, €1 share capital.
- Access to Portugal 2030, IAPMEI, SIFIDE and the Portuguese and European VC
  ecosystem.

**On Brazil:** do not lose the market — **enter it later, as a subsidiary or
through a commercial partnership**, once HidraReview is validated in Portugal. A
Portuguese Lda can sell to Brazil; a Brazilian LTDA cannot apply to NGI Zero. The
asymmetry decides it.

**On Estonia:** it would only make sense if the founders were nomads with no
defined tax residence. That does not appear to be the case, and in that scenario
it creates a problem instead of solving one.
