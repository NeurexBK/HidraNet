# 12 — Company Documents (full checklist)

Requested: *"every document a company should have"*. Here it is, by phase. It
assumes **Portugal, Lda** ([see 11](11-jurisdiction-comparison.md)); the
principles apply to other jurisdictions under different names.

Key: 🔴 required by law · 🟡 required by investors · 🟢 good practice

## G. Specific to a privacy company

These do not appear on any generic checklist, and they are what distinguishes
HidraNet from any other startup:

| | Document | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 | **Transparency report** | Periodic publication of authority requests received and answered. It is the sector standard (Tor, Proton, Mullvad). **Worth more in marketing than any paid campaign.** |
| 🟢 | **Warrant canary** | A periodic statement that no secret requests have been received. Use carefully and with legal advice. |
| 🟢 | **Policy for responding to authority requests** | Write it calmly **before** the first request, never during |
| 🟢 | **Statement of data not collected** | Document by design what HidraNet does **not** keep — it simplifies GDPR and it is a sales argument |
| 🟢 | **Security audit report** | Publish it. Via NGI Zero ([13](13-funds-and-applications.md)) |
| 🟢 | Responsible disclosure policy (security.txt) | Researchers need to know who to report to |
| 🟢 | Published threat model | Say explicitly what HidraNet protects against **and what it does not**. Technical honesty is this community's currency. |
