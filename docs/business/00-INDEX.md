## Honest warnings, before anything else

Three things this package says that you will not enjoy reading, but that are
better read here than heard from an investor:

**1. HidraNet is today two companies inside one repository.**
An anonymity network + browser (an enormous market, almost no willingness to pay,
free competitors far ahead) and HidraReview (a small market, customers who
actually pay). Documents [04](04-business-model.md) and
[05](05-competition.md) treat this seriously. Any VC's first question will be
"which of the two companies are you?" — you need an answer.

**2. The first money should not be VC money.**
It should be non-dilutive. NGI Zero (NLnet) funds exactly this kind of project,
between €5,000 and €50,000, with a light application, deadlines every two months,
and a requirement for open source — which HidraNet already has (MIT). See
[13](13-funds-and-applications.md). This should be submitted at the next
deadline.

**3. Legal risk is not a footnote chapter, it is an architecture decision.**
Whoever operates exit nodes on an anonymity network inherits abuse complaints and
legal requests. The decision that the company will *not* operate the network —
only sell software and services — has to be taken before incorporation, not
after. See [10](10-legal-and-regulatory-risk.md).

## What this package is not

- **It is not legal advice.** Documents 10, 11 and 12 prepare you for the
  conversation with a solicitor; they do not replace it. An anonymity network has
  particularities that require a solicitor who understands digital law.
- **It is not tax or accounting advice.** The projections in document 07 are a
  model with declared assumptions, for discussion — not a forecast.
- **The market numbers are real and sourced.** Where I could not verify
  something, it says so. The numbers *for our business* are assumptions, and they
  are marked as such.
