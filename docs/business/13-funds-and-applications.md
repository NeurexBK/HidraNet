## 1. NGI Zero (NLnet) — **top priority**

### Why it is an almost perfect fit

| NGI Zero requirement | HidraNet |
|---|---|
| Free/open source | ✅ MIT licence |
| Contributes to the next generation internet | ✅ Onion routing, DHT, decentralised network |
| Privacy and trust technology | ✅ It is literally the product |
| A common good, not a proprietary product | ✅ Public repository |
| Applicants may be **individuals** | ✅ **No company required** |

### Numbers

- **Amount:** €5,000 to €50,000 per project, with the possibility of a
  significant increase where potential is demonstrated.
- **Available funds:** NGI Zero Commons Fund with **€21.6M** through 2027; NGI
  Zero Entrust with **€9.6M** dedicated to privacy and trust technologies — the
  latter is the one most aligned with HidraNet.
- **Deadlines:** consecutive open calls, with a deadline every two months.
  *(Confirm the exact date at nlnet.nl — I could not reach the site from this
  environment to check the current calendar.)*
- **Dilution: zero.** It is a grant, not equity.

### Extras worth as much as the money

Grantees receive, at no cost: **security audits**, accessibility audits,
mentoring, testing support and advice on licensing and copyright.

**The security audit is the most valuable item.** An external audit of a privacy
browser costs tens of thousands of euros on the market and is a credibility
prerequisite before any public launch
([06, section 2](06-go-to-market.md)). Getting it through NGI Zero removes a
critical-path blocker for free.

### Which project to submit

**Do not submit "HidraNet".** Too broad. Submit a specific, technical, verifiable
component. Two strong proposals:

1. **"Post-quantum onion routing"** — `hidra-node` with ML-KEM-1024. Concrete,
   technically differentiating, exactly the Entrust mandate. **This is the
   strongest proposal.**
2. **"A privacy browser with verifiable anonymity"** — independent exit IP
   verification + the kill switch. A user-protection angle.

### How to write it

NGI Zero values technical clarity over commercial language. The application is
short. Write for an engineer, not an investor:
- What is being built, technically
- Why it is a common good and not one company's product
- How the result is measured
- Budget per task
- Licence and where the code is

**Mistake to avoid:** talking about business model, revenue or HidraReview. That
belongs in the VC deck, and in this context it only hurts.

## 2. EIC Accelerator — for later

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Grant | up to **€2.5M** (lump sum, TRL 6–8, up to 24 months) |
| Equity | **€1–10M** (the equity minimum rose from €0.5M in 2025 to €1M in 2026) |
| STEP Scale-up | up to €30M |
| Eligibility | Startup/SME in a Member State or associated country |
| 2026 deadlines (step 2) | 7 Jan · 4 Mar · 6 May · 8 Jul · 2 Sep · 4 Nov |
| Step 1 (short proposal) | **No deadline — submittable at any time** |

**Realism:** the EIC is extremely competitive and expects TRL 6–8 with market
validation. HidraNet is not there today. **But the step 1 short proposal has no
deadline and is free** — it is worth submitting to get the assessment, which is
useful feedback regardless of the outcome.

**Realistic target: 2027**, after HidraReview revenue and a completed NGI Zero.

## 4. VC — when and who

**When:** not before having (a) NGI Zero approved or submitted, (b) 50–100 paying
businesses on HidraReview, (c) churn measured over at least 3 months.

Without that, the pitch is an idea with two founders — in 2026, with European
seeds of €1–5M demanding demonstrable traction, it does not get through.

**Market context:** the capital exists. Cybersecurity and privacy raised $10.6bn
in 2026; $4.4bn in Q2 alone. The bar rose along with it.

**Investor profile to look for:**
1. European pre-seed/seed VCs with a thesis in privacy or infrastructure
2. Angel investors from the privacy and open-source world — smarter money than
   generic capital in this area
3. **Avoid** generalist VCs with no privacy thesis: they will push for data
   monetisation, which is incompatible with the product

**Amount to ask for:** €300–700k pre-seed is the credible range for "prove the
HidraReview sales engine in Portugal". Asking for €2M with no revenue is not
ambition — it is not knowing the market.
