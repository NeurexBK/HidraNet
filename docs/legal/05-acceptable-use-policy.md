# Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) — HidraNet

**Version 1.0 · In force from [DATE]**
An integral part of the Terms and Conditions of Use.

---

## 1. Principle

HidraNet exists so that people can communicate privately. Privacy is a right,
recognised in Article 12 of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights and
Article 8 of the European Convention on Human Rights.

Privacy is not impunity. This policy defines what is not permitted.

---

## 2. Prohibitions

### 2.1 Exploitation of minors

**An absolute prohibition, with no exception.** It is prohibited to produce,
distribute, access or facilitate access to child sexual abuse material, or to use
the network to groom minors.

There is no interpretation, context or purpose that makes this acceptable.
Reports: **abuse@hidranet.org**.

### 2.2 Attacks on computer systems

It is prohibited to use the network to:
- Access third-party systems without authorisation
- Carry out denial-of-service attacks
- Scan for or exploit vulnerabilities without authorisation
- Distribute malware, ransomware or command-and-control tooling
- Collect credentials by deception (phishing)

**Exception:** security testing **with written authorisation** from the system's
owner, or within a public vulnerability disclosure programme.

### 2.3 Unsolicited bulk messaging

It is prohibited to send spam or unsolicited commercial mail, or to automate bulk
sending through the network.

### 2.4 Fraud

It is prohibited to use the network for fraud, fraudulent financial schemes,
identity theft or money laundering.

### 2.5 Fake reviews (HidraReview)

It is prohibited to publish reviews that do not correspond to real experience, to
buy or sell reviews, or to publish reviews about your own business or a
competitor's with the intention of manipulating them.

### 2.6 Unlawful content

It is prohibited to use the network to distribute content whose distribution is a
crime in the applicable jurisdiction, in particular incitement to violence and
credible threats against identified people.

### 2.7 Copyright infringement

It is prohibited to use the services we operate to distribute protected works
without authorisation.

### 2.8 Abuse of the infrastructure

It is prohibited to:
- Run an open proxy from a node, by exposing the proxy outside loopback
- Exploit vulnerabilities in the network itself instead of reporting them
- Operate nodes with the intention of de-anonymising other participants
- Circumvent the technical limits of paid services

---

## 3. What we are able to do

Let us be honest about the limits, because a policy that promises the impossible
is taken seriously by nobody.

### 3.1 What we **cannot** do

| | Why |
|---|---|
| Read encrypted messages | We do not have the keys |
| Identify who sends what | No such record exists |
| Remove content from third-party nodes | We do not operate them |
| Block someone from the network | The network is decentralised, by design |

**This is not reluctance — it is architecture.** The same property that protects
the journalist protects whoever abuses it. It is the inherent cost of any privacy
tool, and we acknowledge it rather than hide it.

### 3.2 What we can do

| | |
|---|---|
| Suspend HidraReview accounts | ✅ |
| Remove content from our servers | ✅ |
| Stop providing paid services | ✅ |
| Cooperate with authorities, within the law and within what we hold | ✅ |
| Fix vulnerabilities that enable abuse | ✅ |
| Refuse to provide a service | ✅ |

## 4. Reports

**abuse@hidranet.org**

Include:
1. A description of what you observed
2. The URL or address, if applicable
3. Date and time
4. A contact, in case we need clarification

Child abuse material: report it to the competent authorities as well — the
**Polícia Judiciária** (Portugal) or **INHOPE** (international). There is no need
to wait for us.

### 4.1 How we handle them

| Timeframe | Action |
|---|---|
| 24 hours | We acknowledge receipt |
| 72 hours | We review and decide |
| Immediate | Cases of child abuse or imminent risk to people |

---

## 5. Consequences

| Severity | Measure |
|---|---|
| First minor breach | Warning |
| Repeat breach | Service suspension |
| Serious breach | Immediate termination, no refund |
| Criminal offence | Termination and report to the authorities, as the law requires |

---

## 6. Appeals

Anyone who disagrees with a decision may appeal within **30 days** to
**appeals@hidranet.org**, stating the grounds.

We respond within 14 days. Appeals are reviewed by someone other than the person
who took the initial decision.

---

## 7. Transparency Report

We publish **every six months**:

- The number of reports received, by category
- The number of actions taken
- The number of authority requests received, by type
- The number of requests acted upon
- The number of appeals and their outcome

We publish the numbers; we do not publish data that identifies people.

---

## 8. Changes

Changes are communicated **30 days** in advance, unless required by law or
necessary to stop ongoing abuse.
