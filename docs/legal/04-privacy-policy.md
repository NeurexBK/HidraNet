## 3. Data we do **not** process

Stated expressly, because it is central:

- ❌ Browsing history
- ❌ IP addresses of browser users
- ❌ HidraChat or HidraMail message content
- ❌ Private cryptographic keys
- ❌ Search queries (HidraSearch does not log them)
- ❌ Advertising or tracking identifiers
- ❌ Biometric or location data

**This is a property of the design.** Encryption happens in the client and the
keys never leave the device. Even if we wanted to — or were compelled to — we
could not hand over the content, because we do not hold it.

## 8. Security

Measures applied:

- End-to-end encryption in the client, so that we do not hold the content
- Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest on our servers
- Access restricted to the minimum necessary, with multi-factor authentication
- Logging of administrative access
- An Information Security Policy and an Incident Response Plan
- An independent external security audit **planned** — the report will be
  published

**In the event of a data breach** involving high risk, we notify the supervisory
authority within **72 hours** and the affected data subjects without undue delay.
The procedure is in the Incident Response Plan.
